Questions Johannesburg buyers type

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Johannesburg?

Johannesburg cleaners usually quote per site visit or on a monthly contract priced by floor area, cleaning frequency and tasks — there is no useful single price. Tap Contact and ask two or three companies for a written quote that lists exactly what is included per visit.

What does a commercial cleaning contract include?

Typically: daily or weekly office cleaning, ablutions, kitchens, dusting, vacuuming and waste removal — plus optional deep cleans, window cleaning and hygiene services. The Johannesburg companies on this page cover offices, retail, industrial and hospitality sites; confirm each item is in the contract before you sign, then tap Contact to call.

Do Johannesburg cleaning companies work after hours?

Many do — several companies on this page advertise after-hours and weekend schedules so offices are cleaned outside working hours. Do not assume your hours are covered; tap Contact to call and ask about after-hours availability for your site.
