Questions Manila buyers type

How much does managed IT support cost for a small business in Manila?

Most Manila MSPs quote per project or on a monthly retainer based on device count and support hours — there is no useful single price. Tap Contact and ask two or three shops for a written quote covering what is included: helpdesk hours, response times, and what costs extra.

What should an IT support contract cover for my office?

At minimum: helpdesk support with stated response times, proactive monitoring, data backup, and network and device security. The Manila shops on this page also handle email systems, servers, and on-site visits — confirm each item is in the contract before you sign, then tap Contact to call.

Do Manila IT support companies do on-site visits?

Many do — several shops on this page advertise on-site support alongside remote help across Metro Manila. Do not assume your area is covered; tap Contact to call and ask about on-site response times for your location.
