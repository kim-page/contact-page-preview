Questions Manchester buyers type

Who do I call for a commercial EICR in Manchester?

Call a commercial electrical contractor, not a domestic call-out desk. The Manchester shops on this page do Electrical Installation Condition Reports for offices, retail units, and industrial premises. Read the notes, then tap Contact to call — ask what the report covers and how fast the certificate comes back.

How much does commercial electrical work cost in Manchester?

Most commercial jobs are quoted per project, not by the hour: ask for an itemised written quote covering materials, labour, and certification. Rates rise with out-of-hours and three-phase work. Tap Contact to call two or three shops and compare the quote, not just the day rate.

Do Manchester commercial electricians offer emergency callouts?

Some do — several shops on this page advertise 24/7 emergency response for business premises. Don’t assume; tap Contact to call and ask who actually answers at night, and what the out-of-hours charge is before they roll a van.

How do I check an electrician’s credentials in the UK?

Ask whether they’re NICEIC or ECA approved and request the certificate number, then verify it on the scheme’s own website. For commercial work also confirm they issue completion certificates. Then tap Contact to call the shop.
